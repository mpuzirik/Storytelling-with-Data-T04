# Week 4 - Storytelling Part I

## 1. Understand the Context

### WHO is our audience?

Our main audience is the CEO and management of Bean There Done That.

The CEO wants to understand whether the company is operationally ready to grow again and which improvements should be made before accepting more customers.

### WHAT do we want to communicate?

Our main business question is:

Is Bean There Done That operationally ready to grow next year, and which operational improvements are needed before expanding?

We will use the company's 2023 data and KPIs to evaluate current performance, identify strengths and improvement opportunities, investigate possible growth roadblocks, and make evidence-based recommendations.

### HOW will we communicate the story?

We will present a small number of focused charts supported by clear explanations. The presentation will introduce the business situation, show key evidence, explain potential risks, recommend actions and conclude whether management has enough information to make a growth decision.

## 2. Big Idea

The analysis identifies several issues that management should address or investigate:

- **Inventory and waste:** The dataset records 48,000 kg of finished coffee scrapped, concentrated in June, July and August. June accounts for 21,000 kg.
- **Production quality:** The recorded batch rejection rate is 2.54% for Probat Px120 compared with 1.28% for Probat P60. Operating conditions may differ, so this does not establish the cause.
- **Sales data reliability:** The e-commerce dataset contains 119,101 exact duplicate rows, but no unique order IDs. It is not known whether these are duplicate errors or legitimate identical transactions.
- **Delivery performance:** Average e-commerce delivery time is 1.59 calendar days, reaching 1.91 calendar days in December.
- **Production capacity:** Estimated gross roasting output is 795,671.2 kg, including output associated with rejected batches. Available spare capacity cannot be confirmed from the workbook alone.

### Working Big Idea

Before committing to further growth, Bean There Done That should validate its sales records, investigate waste and quality differences, and establish whether production and delivery operations can support additional demand.

This is a preliminary management recommendation, not proof that the company cannot grow.
