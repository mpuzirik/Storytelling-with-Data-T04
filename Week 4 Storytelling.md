# Week 4 - Storytelling Part I

## 1. Understand the Context

### WHO is our audience?

Our main audience is the CEO and management of Bean There Done That.

The CEO wants to know whether the company is operationally ready to grow again and what should be improved before accepting more customers.

### WHAT do we want to communicate?

Our main question is:

Is Bean There Done That operationally ready for further growth?

We want to use the company data and KPIs to:

- evaluate current performance
- identify areas that perform well
- identify operational bottlenecks
- identify problems that could become worse if the company grows
- recommend actions to prepare the company for future growth

The final conclusions will be based on the results of the data analysis.

### HOW will we communicate the story?

We will use clear visualizations supported by short explanations.

The presentation will:

1. Introduce the current situation
2. Show current operational performance
3. Present the most important KPI findings
4. Show the main bottlenecks
5. Explain what could happen if the company grows
6. Give recommendations
7. Show the expected outcome
8. Give a final conclusion about growth readiness

## 2. Big Idea

The final Big Idea will be confirmed after the KPI analysis is complete.

Working Big Idea:

Bean There Done That has opportunities to resume growth, but we first need to determine whether the current operations can support additional demand without creating new bottlenecks.

The final Big Idea will be based on the most important findings from the data analysis.


## 3. Storyboard

The final presentation will follow this structure:

1. Introduce Bean There Done That and explain why growth readiness is being investigated.
2. Show the current operational situation using the most important KPIs.
3. Highlight the areas that are currently performing well.
4. Present the main operational bottlenecks or improvement opportunities.
5. Explain what could happen to these areas if demand increases.
6. Recommend actions to remove or reduce the identified bottlenecks.
7. Show the expected effect of the recommendations where possible.
8. Conclude whether Bean There Done That is ready for further growth.
