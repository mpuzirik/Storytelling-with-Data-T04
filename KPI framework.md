# KPI Framework

The KPI framework defines the indicators used to evaluate Bean there Done That's operational performance and identify potential barriers to growth.

| Operational Area | KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Sourcing | Raw material stock level | Measure availability of raw materials |
| Sourcing | Raw material stock value | Measure capital tied up in inventory |
| Sourcing | Scrapped raw materials | Measure material waste |
| Production | Production output | Measure production volume |
| Production | Rejected batches | Measure production quality |
| Production | Rejection rate | Measure production efficiency |
| Warehouse | Finished product stock | Measure available inventory |
| Warehouse | Scrapped finished product | Measure product waste |
| E-commerce | Sales records | Measure e-commerce activity |
| E-commerce | Sales value | Measure e-commerce revenue |
| E-commerce | Quantity sold | Measure product demand |
| Delivery | Average delivery time | Measure delivery performance |
| Delivery | Shipping costs | Measure delivery costs |
| B2B | Sales records | Measure B2B activity |
| B2B | Sales value | Measure B2B revenue |
| B2B | Quantity sold | Measure B2B demand |

## KPI Selection

The KPIs were selected based on the main business question and the operational areas identified during the Business Understanding stage.

The KPIs will be used to:

- identify operational strengths and weaknesses;
- detect potential bottlenecks;
- understand demand and sales development;
- evaluate waste and production quality;
- assess whether current operations may constrain future growth.

The KPI framework will be refined if further analysis identifies additional indicators that are necessary to answer the main business question.
